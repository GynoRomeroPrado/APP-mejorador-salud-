# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: November 2024
**Effective Date**: November 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to Health & Fitness App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, full name, date of birth
- **Profile Data**: Height, weight, gender, fitness goals, experience level
- **Authentication Data**: Password (encrypted), OAuth tokens (Google, Apple)

### 1.2 Health & Fitness Data
- **Workout Data**: Exercise logs, sets, reps, weight lifted, workout duration
- **Medication Data** (Encrypted): Medication names, dosages, schedules, adherence logs
- **Body Measurements**: Weight tracking, body composition (if provided)
- **Personal Records**: Best lifts, performance milestones

### 1.3 Usage Data
- **App Analytics**: Features used, screen views, session duration
- **Device Information**: Device type, OS version, app version
- **Location Data**: Timezone (for medication reminders)

### 1.4 User-Generated Content
- Workout notes, medication notes, profile photos
- Comments on workouts, social interactions

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Provide Services
- Deliver workout tracking and medication reminder functionality
- Generate personalized workout recommendations
- Send scheduled medication notifications
- Display progress tracking and statistics

### 2.2 Improve Our Services
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features (aggregated, anonymized data)
- Train machine learning models for better recommendations
- Identify and fix bugs

### 2.3 Communication
- Send medication and workout reminders
- Notify you of new features or updates
- Respond to support requests

### 2.4 Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect against fraudulent or illegal activity
- Enforce our Terms of Service

## 4. Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy Laws)

### 4.1 Right to Access
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you (JSON export available in-app).

### 4.2 Right to Rectification
Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

### 4.3 Right to Deletion
Request deletion of your account and associated data (Settings > Account > Delete Account).

### 4.4 Right to Data Portability
Export your data in machine-readable format (JSON).

### 4.5 Right to Object
Opt-out of analytics tracking (Settings > Privacy > Analytics).

### 4.6 Right to Restrict Processing
Request limitation of how we process your data.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Encryption
- **At Rest**: AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data (medications)
- **In Transit**: TLS 1.3 for all API communications
- **Passwords**: Bcrypt hashing with salt

### 5.2 Access Controls
- Row-level security in database
- Multi-factor authentication for admin access
- Regular security audits

### 5.3 Data Retention
- Active accounts: Data retained indefinitely
- Deleted accounts: Anonymized within 30 days, some data retained for legal compliance
- Medication logs: 5 years (health record compliance)

## 6. Cookies and Tracking

### 6.1 Essential Cookies
- Authentication session tokens (required for functionality)

### 6.2 Analytics Cookies
- Mixpanel tracking (can be disabled in Settings)
- No advertising or third-party tracking cookies

## 7. Children's Privacy
